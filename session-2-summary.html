Session 2 completed

From procrastination to purposeful action

Ian successfully applied the five-minute start, developed clearer task-management language, practiced distraction recovery, and selected practical systems for the start of school.

A clear first win

Ian completed his first between-session assignment: the laundry challenge. Although he did not initially feel motivated, he remembered the five-minute start, identified the easiest first action, and began. Once he was moving, he found that the rest of the task came easily.

Lesson learned: action can come before motivation. The goal is not to wait until a task feels appealing; it is to make the first step small and visible enough to begin.

Making vague work visible

Ian practiced replacing broad statements such as “I need to get my homework done” with specific actions such as “I need to complete the Algebra assignment due tomorrow.” This creates a defined target, reduces the feeling that the task is insurmountable, and establishes a clear completion point.

Priorities and follow-through

We continued developing Ian’s ability to decide what should happen first, what must begin early, and what can wait. The goal is to make workload decisions intentionally rather than defaulting to whichever task feels easiest in the moment.

Recovering after distraction

Ian learned the sequence Notice → Name → Reset → Resume. He identified his phone and friends as his most likely distractions. One practical strategy is to place his phone in another room during focused work.

Work-block system

We discussed high-performance block scheduling. Ian thoughtfully adapted the model to match his current attention span: approximately 20 minutes of focused work followed by a short break. Starting shorter should support consistency and prevent a distraction from consuming an extended work period.

Tool selected: Todoist

Ian was introduced to Todoist as a free system for recording tasks, assigning priorities, and placing work on a schedule. He liked the platform, downloaded it, and completed the initial setup so it is ready for the beginning of school.
