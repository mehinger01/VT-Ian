The school-control cycle

This cycle is designed to reduce missing work, last-minute emergencies, and the feeling of not knowing where to begin.

Capture → Clarify

Record the task, due date, course, and what it actually requires. Replace “study Chemistry” with a concrete action such as “answer five vocabulary questions without notes.”

Prioritize → Start

Decide what must be finished, what must be started early, and what can wait. Then choose the correct launch strategy.

Recover → Close

Return quickly after distractions. Before leaving a task, mark it complete, submit it, or record exactly when and how you will resume.

3

Turn vague tasks into visible actions

A useful next action is specific enough that another person could watch you do it.

Visible-action test: Could someone watch you complete the exact action right now? If not, it is still too vague.
4

Choose what comes first

Use three factors: urgency, importance, and effort.

Do FirstDue soon, high impact, or blocking other work.
Start EarlyLarge or important work that becomes a crisis if delayed.
Quick CompleteShort, clear, and ready to finish.
MonitorNot urgent yet, but still visible.
5

Estimate the real time

Real estimate: Setup + Work + Check + Submit
6

Select the right start strategy

Five-minute start

Use when you are avoiding, overwhelmed, or waiting for motivation.

Focused work block

Use for work that requires sustained attention and has a defined target.

Quick complete

Use only when the task is genuinely short and all materials are ready.

Multi-day breakdown

Use for essays, tests, projects, and assignments with several stages.

7

Recover after a distraction

Notice → Name → Reset → Resume

Getting distracted does not mean the entire work period is lost.

8

Stress-test the system

Scenario: It is 4:15 p.m. You have a commitment at 6:30. The Chemistry quiz has moved one day earlier, the English reading took longer than expected, and an Engineering assignment was added verbally in class.
9

Close the loop

Before leaving any task, decide which statement is true.

Complete

The work is finished, checked, and submitted.

Scheduled return

The next action and return time are recorded.
